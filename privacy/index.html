Privacy, in plain language.

ICS Rescue does not receive your calendar data while you repair it.

Calendar contents

ICS files and pasted text stay in memory in your browser. They are not uploaded, saved to an account, or placed in browser storage.

Calendar providers

If you open a Google or Outlook link, the URL sends that event’s details to the chosen provider. Apple downloads stay local until you choose where to open them.

Demo data

The demo sample stays in memory. Resetting restores the bundled sample. Starting for real discards the sample.

Site operations

ICS Rescue uses no analytics, ads, tracking pixels, cookies, or third-party runtime scripts. The host may keep ordinary security logs.

Offline files

The service worker caches public interface files and artwork. It does not cache the ICS text you provide.

Contact

Questions can be sent through Sociobot (external).
