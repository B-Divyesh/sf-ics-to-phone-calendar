Privacy, in plain language.

ICS Rescue is designed so we do not receive your calendar data.

Calendar contents

ICS files and pasted text are read and repaired locally by code running in your browser. Calendar contents are not uploaded to us, saved to an account, or placed in browser storage.

Calendar providers

If you choose “Open in Google” or “Open in Outlook,” your browser sends the event details in the provider’s URL. That provider’s privacy policy then applies. Apple downloads remain local files until you choose where to open or share them.

Site operations

This version uses no analytics, advertising, tracking pixels, cookies, or third-party runtime scripts. Our hosting provider may process ordinary network logs such as IP address, URL, user agent, and request time for security and reliability.

Offline cache

A service worker may cache the app’s public interface files and artwork for offline use. It does not cache the calendar content you provide. You can remove this cache through your browser’s site-data controls.

Contact

Questions can be sent through sociobot.in.
